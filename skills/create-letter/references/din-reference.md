# DIN scope and sources

The supplied Google Doc is the layout authority for template-based letters. Its title is not evidence that every measurement conforms to DIN 5008.

DIN Media's catalog lists [DIN 5008:2020-03](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-5008/318422674), with correction DIN 5008 Berichtigung 1:2020-07 (checked 2026-10-05). The standard concerns writing and presentation of text and information, including correspondence. The catalog is not the full normative text.

For a request to audit strict compliance or change to Form A/Form B, verify the relevant provisions from an authoritative source or a user-provided licensed standard. State which measurements and edition were actually checked. Do not infer form or certification from the file title, reproduce the standard, or label every inherited template measurement as a DIN requirement.

Separate three claims: the wording is complete, the native template structure was preserved, and the rendered page geometry was checked. Report only the checks actually performed. German default wording and the specific font/spacing in the template are workflow choices, not a claim that DIN mandates them for all letters.

## Date formats and information-block grouping

DIN 5008 permits more than one date representation. For domestic German correspondence, `DD.MM.YYYY` is valid, with two-digit day/month, a four-digit year, and no spaces: `01.01.2021`. The international numeric form is `YYYY-MM-DD`, for example `2021-01-01`. A written month is also valid: `1. Januar 2021`, without a leading zero on the day. This plugin defaults to the original template's dotted numeric style for domestic letters; that choice is not a claim that DIN requires this one style. Preserve explicit user preferences and use the actual letter date.

Within this template's information block, removing a missing telephone field should leave the remaining contact details contiguous. Blank lines distinguish groups, such as the contact details from the date, rather than reserving space for absent values.

Practical references checked 2026-10-05: [Klett, Regeln der DIN 5008](https://www.klett.de/inhalt/media_fast_path/32/DO01_3-12-007172_Onlinematerialien_DIN5008.pdf), page 9 for dates and page 15 for information-block grouping; [Die Briefprofis, date formats under DIN 5008:2020](https://diebriefprofis-blog.de/datum-korrekt-schreiben-nach-din-5008/), including the domestic-recipient condition for dotted numeric dates. These explanations support these specific choices; they are not a full normative compliance audit.
